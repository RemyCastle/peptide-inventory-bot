================================================================
  LINK YOUR WEBSITE TO YOUR TELEGRAM SHOP
  (Site Links — quick guide for shop admins)
================================================================

Your bot shop can pull its catalog straight from your website.
Prices and product names stay in sync automatically — you stop
retyping them in two places.

----------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOW TO CONNECT
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Option A:  In your shop, open  ⚙️ Admin Panel → 🌐 Site links
             and follow the buttons.

  Option B:  Send this command while in your shop:

               /linksite https://yoursite.com

The bot checks the feed right away and tells you how many
products it found. Up to 5 sites per shop.

  TIP: photos and COAs can be added straight from the panel —
  tap 📷 on a product to upload a photo, and 📄 Upload PDF or
  🔗 Link to attach its COA. No Telegram needed.

----------------------------------------------------------------
2. WHAT YOUR SITE NEEDS TO PROVIDE
----------------------------------------------------------------

A public web address that returns JSON. The bot tries:

    https://yoursite.com/api/products

(If your feed lives at another path, just give the full URL —
anything containing /api/ or ending in .json is used as-is.)

The JSON must look like:

    { "products": [ ...one entry per product... ] }

Each product entry can be in either shape:

  SIMPLE (recommended for most sites):
    {
      "id": 1,                  (or "sku": "ABC-1")
      "name": "BPC-157 10MG",
      "price": 41,
      "kit_price": 294,         optional - price for a kit
      "stock": 25,              optional - see stock rules below
      "unit": "vial",           optional
      "description": "...",     optional
      "active": true,           optional
      "sort_order": 10          optional
    }

  SPBC STOREFRONT shape (springfield-style sites):
    {
      "id": 1,
      "name": "BPC-157 10MG",
      "vial_price": 41,
      "pack_price": 294,
      "kit_only": false,
      "sort_order": 10,
      "active": true
    }

----------------------------------------------------------------
3. WHO OWNS WHAT
----------------------------------------------------------------

  YOUR SITE owns:   names, prices, kit prices, active on/off,
                    display order.

  YOUR BOT owns:    stock counts — UNLESS your feed includes a
                    "stock" number, in which case the bot follows
                    your site's stock.

  New products from the feed arrive with stock 0, so nothing can
  sell until you set real stock (Admin Panel → 📦 Products).

  Products that disappear from your feed are switched OFF in the
  bot, never deleted. Turn the feed entry back on and they return
  with their old stock.

  Products you added by hand in the bot are NEVER touched by the
  sync.

----------------------------------------------------------------
4. WHEN IT SYNCS
----------------------------------------------------------------

  * Automatically every few hours.
  * Instantly when you tap  🔄 Sync now  in Site links.

----------------------------------------------------------------
5. REQUIREMENTS CHECKLIST
----------------------------------------------------------------

  [ ] Public HTTPS address (no login wall)
  [ ] Returns HTTP 200 with JSON
  [ ] Top-level "products" array
  [ ] Every product has a name and a price

  The bot identifies itself with:
    User-Agent:  SPBC-InventoryBot/1.0
    Header:      X-SPBC-Member: 1   (harmless for other sites)

----------------------------------------------------------------
6. TROUBLESHOOTING
----------------------------------------------------------------

  "Site API HTTP 403"
      Your host is blocking bots. Ask them (or your firewall
      settings) to allow the User-Agent "SPBC-InventoryBot/1.0".

  "returned no products list"
      The JSON is missing the top-level "products" array, or the
      URL is returning an HTML page instead of JSON.

  Linked but 0 products found
      Each entry needs at least a name plus a price (simple
      shape) or vial_price/pack_price (SPBC shape).

  Wrong prices showing
      Simple shape: "price" is the per-unit price, "kit_price"
      the kit. SPBC shape: "vial_price" is per vial, "pack_price"
      per 10-pack/kit. Check you filled the right fields.

Questions? Message the shop owner.

----------------------------------------------------------------
7. THE OTHER DIRECTION (PAID WEBSITE ORDERS)
----------------------------------------------------------------

  Catalog sync is site → bot. Paid Springfield PBC orders go the other
  way: when the site posts status paid to POST /notify, this bot creates
  one Unicorn shop order already marked paid (stock deducts like an
  admin confirm). Same order_number will not duplicate. Unmatched SKUs
  stay on the order as a note; nothing is invented in the catalog.
  Set UNICORN_SHOP_CHAT_ID to the Unicorn shop (0 = skip). No extra
  invoice is sent — the customer already paid on the website.
